Settings fill your business. An invoice fills the document, the items, and the tax. A saved client fills the consignee. Distance, vehicle, and the other transport fields are typed here, and you can correct any filled field.

    Document

    Use this when the supply is inward, so your GSTIN is the consignee.

    Consignor (from)

    For Bill From – Dispatch From, the GSTIN and state are the bill-from party. Address, place, PIN and dispatch state are where the goods leave from.

    Consignee (to)

    For Bill To – Ship To, the GSTIN, trade name and state are the bill-to party. Address, place, PIN and ship-to state are where the goods are delivered.
